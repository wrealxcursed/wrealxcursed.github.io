WREAL X

born Angel Jambu Roy · July 20, 2007 · Yei, South Sudan

Wreal X is a South Sudanese singer, rapper, and songwriter whose sound blends dark emotion, Afrobeats, and emo-trap energy.

Born to the late Mr. Taban Roy and Mrs. Santuke Koloy Kamba, he discovered his love for music as a child and began writing lyrics before adolescence.

“Every verse is a confession — a soul marked by suffering and hope.”

His sound carries the intensity of emotional vulnerability, while still echoing the rhythms and warmth of his East African roots. Early releases such as Monalisa and Malice helped establish his name as a rising voice in a generation shaped by pain, faith, and reinvention.
