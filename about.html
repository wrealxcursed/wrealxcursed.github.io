WREAL X

born Angel Jambu Roy · July 20, 2007 · Yei, South Sudan

Wreal X is a South Sudanese singer, emo rapper, and songwriter from East Africa. Deeply influenced by Juice WRLD, he blends raw vulnerability of emo trap with the warmth of Afrobeats.

Born to the late Mr. Taban Roy and Mrs. Santuke Koloy Kamba, Wreal X discovered his passion for music at ten years old and began writing rap songs before understanding the weight of his own words.

"Every verse is a confession — a soul marked by suffering and hope."

His early releases, "Monalisa" and "Photo", carried Afro influences familiar to his homeland, but "Hurry Up" and "Gang Gang" introduced a distinct emo-trap identity rooted in honesty and emotional intensity.
